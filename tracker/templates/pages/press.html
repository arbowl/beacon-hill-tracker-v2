Press

For reporters and researchers

You are welcome to cite the Tracker's figures. A few notes help keep them accurate in print.

Citing the data

  • Name the source as the Beacon Hill Compliance Tracker, and give the date of the data. The date of each committee's data is on the committee table.
  • Describe findings as detected violations, or as cases where the public record does not show a requirement being met. The Tracker does not establish that a committee broke a rule, or why.
  • Most figures count bill segments: one bill as one committee saw it. A bill referred to two committees counts twice. The methodology explains the difference.
  • Every dashboard view has a shareable link. Linking to the exact view you used lets readers check the figure.

Questions

I am happy to walk through how a figure was produced, or to check a specific bill before publication. Use the contact form and mention your deadline.
